## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a proposed information system modernization initiative for Showtime Sound & Lighting (SSL) Inc., a growing business providing event production services, DJ services, equipment rentals, and audio, video, and lighting equipment sales.

The project focuses on identifying operational inefficiencies, gathering business and technical requirements, and designing a centralized information system to improve daily operations, data accuracy, resource management, and customer service.

The deliverable is a systems analysis and design proposal rather than a deployed software application.

## 🎯 Business Problem

SSL relied on outdated, manual information management processes that created several operational challenges:

- Duplicate and inconsistent business records
- Inefficient customer information retrieval
- Equipment inventory and availability tracking difficulties
- Scheduling conflicts involving employees and equipment
- Delays in customer proposals, orders, and invoicing
- Reduced productivity and declining customer service quality

The business identified a potential risk of losing approximately 5–10% of customers over two years if these inefficiencies were not addressed.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

Design a centralized, automated information system capable of supporting:

- **Customer Management:** Maintain customer profiles, transaction history, and outstanding balances.
- **Inventory Management:** Track equipment availability, condition, rentals, and maintenance.
- **Event Scheduling:** Coordinate employees, contractors, equipment, and event reservations.
- **Order Processing:** Generate proposals, convert approved proposals into orders, and produce invoices.
- **Reporting and Analytics:** Provide management with customer, equipment, and operational reports.
- **Access Security:** Protect business information through authentication, encryption, and role-based permissions.

## 📋 Requirements Analysis

The project documents 25 functional requirements and a set of non-functional requirements.

### Functional Requirements

Key requirements include:

- Create, update, and search customer profiles.
- Track equipment availability and maintenance.
- Generate customer proposals and invoices.
- Manage employee and contractor scheduling.
- Detect overlapping event bookings.
- Record payments and outstanding balances.
- Generate business and operational reports.

### Non-Functional Requirements

The proposed system includes performance, usability, scalability, compatibility, and security requirements.

Examples include:

- Respond to user inputs within two seconds.
- Support up to 15 users and more than 200 customers.
- Provide remote and mobile access.
- Back up business information.
- Maintain secure authentication and user activity logs.
- Protect customer information through encryption.
- Automatically end inactive sessions after 10 minutes.
- Restrict access based on user roles.
- Remain within the proposed $15,000 project budget.

## 🔐 Security & Identity Access Management

An important component of the proposed system is protecting sensitive business and customer information.

Security requirements include:

**Authentication**
- Secure user login
- Password complexity requirements
- Automatic session timeouts

**Authorization**
- Role-based access restrictions
- Administrative privileges
- Registered device access controls

**Data Protection**
- Encryption of customer information
- Backup and recovery requirements

**Accountability**
- Logging user sign-ins
- Recording user actions for accountability

These requirements demonstrate the integration of cybersecurity principles into information systems design.

## 📈 Expected Business Value

The proposed system was designed to deliver several business benefits:

- Reduce manual administrative work.
- Minimize scheduling and inventory errors.
- Improve customer response times.
- Support more accurate operational reporting.
- Improve customer satisfaction and retention.
- Support continued organizational growth.

These benefits represent anticipated outcomes of the proposed system, not measured post-implementation results.

## ⚠️ Project Constraints & Risks

The analysis identified several implementation considerations:

- Maximum proposed budget of $15,000
- Integration with existing business tools
- Employee resistance to operational changes
- Potential data migration errors
- Training and system adoption requirements
- Support for remote access and business growth
